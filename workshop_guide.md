## Introduction

Visual inferential privacy concerns information that AI can infer from images or video even when a user has not explicitly shared it. For example, a medicine bottle and an appointment reminder may suggest a health condition.

The design goal is to help users notice an inference, understand its evidence, and decide how to respond. Masking objects or controlling camera access alone may leave other clues available for inference.

## Design task

Recall a real situation involving visual privacy, such as sharing a photo, wearing smart glasses, or joining a video meeting. Propose **at least two interface or interaction ideas** for that situation. You may choose any device or platform.

Organize each proposal using the four stages below. Text, sketches, flowcharts, interface images, and screenshots are accepted; include at least one image if possible.
