# Speed-Dating Guide

## Procedure

1. Read the study information, provide consent, and review the introduction to inferential privacy.
2. Complete the comprehension questions about inferred information and privacy control.
3. View and try **three assigned designs from D1–D6**, one at a time. For each design, inspect the privacy risk and its evidence, then choose a privacy action.
4. Answer the six rating questions and the open question after each design.
5. Rank the three designs and explain the ranking.

The six interfaces are available in the [prototype folder](prototype/README.md).

## Per-design questionnaire

Answer based on the interaction you just completed. Choose one response from **1–5** for each item.

| Item | English wording | Response anchors |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Can you relate to the user’s privacy concern in this story? | 1 = Not at all; 5 = Completely |
| Q2 | This notification approach helps me understand what the system may infer about me | 1 = Strongly disagree; 5 = Strongly agree |
| Q3 | This notification approach makes me feel able to control privacy risks | 1 = Strongly disagree; 5 = Strongly agree |
| Q4 | This design would not interrupt the task I am currently completing | 1 = Strongly disagree; 5 = Strongly agree |
| Q5 | I would trust a system that uses this notification approach | 1 = Strongly disagree; 5 = Strongly agree |
| Q6 | Overall, this is a good notification approach | 1 = Strongly disagree; 5 = Strongly agree |

**Open response**

Why is this notification approach effective or ineffective? What situations suit it?

[Free-text response]

Complete all six ratings and the open response.
